About

Who I am

I design with passion and code with precision. I started as a fine artist, earning a university gold medal for my Master of Fine Arts, and that eye for craft still drives every interface I build.

Over 20 years I've led UX/UI at Accenture and TCS, taking more than 30 client projects from ideation to production. Today I lead the UX/UI Centre of Excellence practice at TCS, exploring how Gen AI and AI agents change the way people work with software.

I'm still hands-on with front-end code, and outside work you'll find me behind a camera, making crafts or sketching logos.
